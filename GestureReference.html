Active Controls

The 4 Core Spatial Gestures

Zero gesture overlap: Aiming, Scrolling, Grabbing, and Resetting are 100% distinct.

🪄
Aiming & Pointing

1-Finger: 3D Neon Laser Beam

⚡ Aim with Glowing Cyber Laser

Extend your index finger forward. A glowing electric-cyan 3D laser beam shoots out from your hand to the VR screen with a glowing target reticle ring on the landing surface.

  • Point index finger forward (keep middle finger relaxed/curled).
  • The laser smoothly tracks your hand movements across the entire 16:9 monitor.
Tech: Dynamic 3D SCNCylinder oriented via simd_quatf + SCNHitTest.
👌
Clicking

Thumb Tap: Electric Flash Click

🖱️ Pinpoint Click with Visual Confirmation

While pointing your 3D laser beam at any button, tap your thumb against your middle finger (or index finger). The laser beam flashes with electric white light and fires an instant click.

  • Aim the laser dot at any link or video.
  • Tap thumb to middle finger (your index finger stays locked in place).
  • Laser pulses with an electric flash confirmation.
Tech: lastGoodCursorPosition lock + dual-stage visual flash action.
✌️
Web Navigation

2-Finger: Smooth Velocity Scroll

📜 Scroll Pages Like a Trackpad

Extend both your index AND middle fingers (peace sign ✌️). Move your hand up or down to scroll the webpage with natural speed and momentum.

  • Hold up 2 fingers (Index + Middle extended).
  • Move hand up to scroll up, move hand down to scroll down.
  • Laser pointer automatically pauses in this mode so you never trigger accidental clicks!
Tech: Dual-landmark vector delta with 30Hz throttled window.scrollBy.
✊
Spatial Window Move

Closed Fist: Grab & Projector Depth

📦 Drag & Resize in 3D Space

Close all fingers into a tight fist with thumb folded. You now have physical hold of the VR window.

  • Move Left/Right/Up/Down: Repositions window in your room.
  • Move Fist Closer to Camera: Brings window closer and larger like a projector.
  • Move Fist Farther: Pushes window back and smaller.
Tech: Full hand curl lock + palmSpan depth ratio calculator.
👍
Emergency Recalibrate

Thumbs Up Reset (Hold 1.5s)

🔄 Reset Center & Open Home Desktop

Make a clear thumbs-up gesture (thumb straight up, all 4 fingers curled) and hold it for 1.5 seconds.

  • Immediately centers the window in front of your eyes.
  • Resets scale to default 1.0x and opens MadhurVision OS Home.
Tech: Dedicated thumb pose validator with 1.5s hold confirmation.
👀
Head Tracking

120Hz Stereoscopic Head Gaze

🌐 360° Rotational Navigation

Turn your head naturally in any direction inside your VR headset. The entire mixed-reality spatial environment rotates seamlessly with zero latency.

  • Smooth 120 FPS tracking via iPhone CoreMotion attitude sensors.
Tech: CMMotionManager.xArbitraryZVertical.

💡 Pro Tips for Best Tracking Experience

🪄 Laser Aiming

Extend 1 finger to aim your laser beam. The glowing target ring on the screen shows you the exact spot it will click.

✌️ Scrolling vs Pointing

Switch to 2 fingers whenever you want to scroll a webpage. This completely disables the laser so you can scroll freely without clicking anything.

🔄 Lost the Window?

If the window ever goes too far to the side, hold a Thumbs Up 👍 for 1.5s to snap it straight in front of you.

📂 Error Logs

Open the Files app ➡️ On My iPhone ➡️ MadhurVision ➡️ error_log.txt to view real-time camera and tracking diagnostics.
