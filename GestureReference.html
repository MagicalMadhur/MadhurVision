Supported Gestures & Inputs

All inputs below are fully active in the MadhurVision iOS app (Standalone VR mode).

Active

Head Gaze

👀 Look to Navigate

Simply look in any direction inside the VR BOX headset. The 3D world rotates with your head at 120Hz via the iPhone's built-in gyroscope and accelerometer.

Tech: CMMotionManager at 1/120s → SCNNode.eulerAngles updated every frame.
Active

Index Point (2.5x Sensitivity)

⚡ Aim the Virtual Cursor

Extend your index finger. A glowing dot appears on the panel. Thanks to the 2.5x sensitivity multiplier, small hand movements near the center of your view can reach the edges of the VR browser without exiting the camera's FOV.

Tech: VNHumanHandPoseObservation.indexTip → 3D raycast with 2.5x multiplier & 0.05 low-pass filter.
Active

Strict Pinch

🖱️ Click / Tap

Bring your index fingertip and thumb tip together while keeping your middle and ring fingers explicitly straight. This fires a perfect JS mousedown/mouseup click at your cursor location.

Tech: pinchDist < 0.05 && middleTip.y > middleMCP.y. Fires native JS MouseEvents.
✊
Active

Fist Clench

✊ Grab & Move Window

Make a closed fist (curl index, middle, and ring fingers). This locks onto the floating browser. Move your fist around to drag the entire YouTube window to a new location in 3D space.

Tech: All fingertips below MCP joints → offsets 3D node position with 2.5x tracking multiplier.
Active

Palm Swipe Up

⬆️ Scroll Up

Hold your palm open and move it upward to scroll the browser upward.

Tech: Wrist landmark Y-delta. JS: window.scrollBy(0, -delta)
Active

Palm Swipe Down

⬇️ Scroll Down

Hold your palm open and move it downward to scroll the browser down.

Tech: Wrist Y-delta, negative direction. JS: window.scrollBy(0, +delta)
⌨️
Active

VR Air Keyboard

⌨️ Type in Browser

When you click on any text input (e.g., YouTube search), a beautiful frosted-glass virtual keyboard slides up. Pinch the keys to type!

Tech: DOM Injection via WKUserScript. Dispatches JS Input events seamlessly.
🖱️
Active

Bluetooth Mouse (Native & AssistiveTouch)

🖱️ Backup Controller

Connect a mouse! If using Native iOS 14+ Mouse, the iOS pointer hides and a 3D Windows pointer appears. If using AssistiveTouch, simply use the round cursor to click the screen to trigger 3D raycasts.

Tech: UIPanGestureRecognizer (indirectPointer) + Screen Tap Fallbacks.
