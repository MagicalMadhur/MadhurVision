Supported Gestures

All gestures below are currently live in the MadhurVision iOS app (Standalone VR mode).

Active

Head Gaze

👀 Look to Navigate

Simply look in any direction inside the VR BOX headset. The 3D world rotates with your head at 120Hz via the iPhone's built-in gyroscope and accelerometer. The crosshair stays fixed at the center of your vision.

Tech: CMMotionManager at 1/120s → SCNNode.eulerAngles updated every frame. No camera required.
Active

Index Point

⚡ Aim the Virtual Cursor

Extend your index finger and point it toward the floating browser. A glowing cyan dot appears on the panel showing exactly where your finger aims.

Tech: VNHumanHandPoseObservation.indexTip → 3D raycast → SCNHitTest on browser plane.
Active

Pinch

🖱️ Click / Tap

Bring your index fingertip and thumb tip together. This fires a click at the position your cursor is aiming at on the floating browser window.

Tech: indexTip–thumbTip distance < 6% of frame. 500ms cooldown. JS: element.click()
Active

Palm Swipe Up

⬆️ Scroll Up

Hold your palm open and move it upward. The floating browser scrolls up — like pulling a web page upward with your finger on a touchscreen.

Tech: Wrist landmark Y-delta between frames. Threshold: Δy > 0.5%. JS: window.scrollBy(0, -delta)
Active

Palm Swipe Down

⬇️ Scroll Down

Hold your palm open and move it downward. The floating browser scrolls down to reveal more content below — more YouTube videos, longer articles, etc.

Tech: Same wrist Y-delta, negative direction. JS: window.scrollBy(0, +delta)
