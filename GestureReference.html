Active Gestures

Core Hand Gestures & Spatial Controls

All gesture tracking runs with Apple Vision Human Hand Pose + SceneKit 120Hz render pipeline.

👆
Pointing

Index Finger Point

⚡ Aim the High-Contrast Cursor

Extend your index finger forward. A bold white pointer arrow with a thick black border and shadow instantly appears on the VR monitor.

  • Keep index finger extended naturally.
  • Small movements near the center navigate the entire 16:9 monitor thanks to 2.5x sensitivity boost.
Tech: VNHumanHandPose.indexTip with responsive 0.20 filter + 3D raycast.
👌
Click / Tap (Recommended)

Thumb-to-Middle Tap

🖱️ Instant Click Without Cursor Jitter

While pointing at a button or link with your index finger, simply tap your thumb against your middle finger. Your index finger stays completely still, guaranteeing pinpoint click precision.

  • Point index finger at the target.
  • Tap thumb to middle finger to click.
  • Cursor pulses with a click-pop animation.
Tech: hypot(thumb, middleTip) < 0.065. Index stays locked in space.
🤏
Alternative Click

Classic Index Pinch

🖱️ Freeze & Click

Pinch your thumb and index fingertips together. The tracking engine automatically freezes the cursor position the millisecond the pinch motion starts, preventing the pointer from dropping down.

  • Aim at your target with index finger.
  • Pinch index to thumb to fire a click.
Tech: lastGoodCursorPosition lock during pinch cooldown.
✊
Spatial Manipulation

Closed Fist Clench & Projector Depth

📦 Grab, Move & Scale in 3D Depth

Close all fingers into a tight fist with thumb folded. You now have physical hold of the VR monitor. Move your fist in any 3D direction to position it.

  • Move Left / Right / Up / Down: Drags the panel across your room.
  • Move Fist Closer to Camera: Brings window closer & larger like a projector.
  • Move Fist Farther: Pushes window back & smaller.
Tech: Full hand curl detection + palmSpan depth ratio calculator.
👍
Emergency Recalibrate

Thumbs Up Reset (Hold 1.5s)

🔄 Reset Position, Scale & Go Home

Make a clear thumbs-up gesture (thumb extended straight up, all 4 other fingers curled into a fist) and hold it for 1.5 seconds.

  • Instantly centers the monitor directly in front of your gaze.
  • Resets the monitor distance to default (2.0m) and scale to 1.0x.
  • Navigates back to the MadhurVision OS Home desktop.
Tech: Unique thumb-up pose check with 1.5s hold timer to prevent accidental triggers.
👀
120Hz Gyroscope

Stereoscopic Head Gaze

🌐 360° Rotational Navigation

Turn your head naturally in any direction inside your VR headset. The entire mixed-reality spatial environment rotates seamlessly with zero latency.

  • Smooth 120 FPS tracking via iPhone CoreMotion attitude sensors.
  • Independent left and right eye cameras matching physical IPD.
Tech: CMMotionManager.xArbitraryZVertical with delta attitude calibration.
📷
Passthrough MR

Zero-Latency Dual-Eye Passthrough

👁️ Real-World Camera Feed

The rear camera feed is rendered directly inside SceneKit's background scene buffer, delivering smooth stereoscopic camera passthrough to both eyes simultaneously.

  • Can be toggled On/Off in System Settings or via bottom dock.
  • Auto-rotates to match landscape insertion into your VR BOX headset.
Tech: Zero-copy CIImage(cvPixelBuffer:) into scene.background.contents.
⌨️
Spatial Input

VR Air Keyboard

💬 Typing in Browser & Search

Whenever you click on any text box, Google search, or YouTube input, a spatial frosted-glass air keyboard slides up from the bottom of the monitor.

  • Point and click on any key to type directly into the webpage.
  • Click outside the keyboard or press ↵ Search to submit and dismiss.
Tech: WKUserScript DOM injection with native JS InputEvent dispatch.

💡 Pro Tips for Best Tracking Experience

🎯 Steady Hand Clicking

Use the Thumb-to-Middle Tap for clicking buttons! Because your index finger doesn't flex, your aim won't shift during the click.

☀️ Lighting & Contrast

Ensure your room has adequate lighting so the iPhone's camera can clearly distinguish your hand landmarks and gestures.

🔄 Quick Reset

If you ever move the window too far or lose track of it, hold a Thumbs Up 👍 for 1.5s to snap everything right in front of your eyes.

📐 Headset Alignment

The app auto-detects landscape orientation dynamically. Insert your phone into your VR BOX headset in either landscape direction.
